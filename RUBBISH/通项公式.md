14.

由原式$a_{n+1}=a_{n-1}^2-2a_{n-1}-2a_{n}$变形，有$a_{n+1}+2a_{n}=a_{n-1}^2-2a_{n-1}$.
注意到奇数项$a_{2k-1}$影响下一个奇偶对$(a_{2k+1}, a_{2k})$，偶数项$a_{2k}$影响下一个偶奇对$(a_{2k+2}, a_{2k+1})$.
所以其通项$a_{n}$奇偶项形式不同.

由于原式中有$a_{n-1}^2$，考虑$\cos$的倍角公式，联想到复数平方->角度翻倍，进而想到其复数形式.

由于原式是二阶递推，考虑$u_{n}, v_{n}$.
$u_{n}=\alpha^{2^n}+\bar{\alpha}^{2^n} = 2\cos(2^n\phi_{1}), v_{n}=\beta^{2^n}+\bar{\beta}^{2^n} = 2\cos(2^n\phi_{2})$，其中$\alpha=e^{i\phi_{1}},  \beta=e^{i\phi_{2}},|\alpha|=|\beta|=1$.
有$u_{n}^2=(\alpha^{2^n}+\bar{\alpha}^{2^n})^2=\alpha^{2^{n+1}}+\bar{\alpha}^{2^{n+1}}+2=u_{n+1}+2$. $v_{n}$同理.
注意到，
$$a_n = {
\begin{cases}u_{\frac{n+1}{2}}+v_{\frac{n+1}{2}}+1, n为奇数 \\ 
 u_{\frac{n}{2}} \cdot v_{\frac{n}{2}}+1, n为偶数 \\
\end{cases}
}$$
满足：
当$n=2k-1(k \in \mathbb{N^+})$时，$a_{n+1}=a_{n-1}^2-2a_{n-1}-2a_{n}$
$$LHS=a_{2k}=u_{k}v_{k}+1, RHS=(u_{k-1}v_{k-1}+1)^2-2(u_{k-1}v_{k-1}+1)-2(u_{k}+v_{k}+1)=(u^2_{k-1}-2)(v^2_{k-1}-2)+1=u_{k}v_{k}+1$$
当$n=2k(k \in \mathbb{N^+})$时，$a_{n+1}=a_{n-1}^2-2a_{n-1}-2a_{n}$
$$LHS=a_{2k+1}=u_{k}+v_{k}+1,RHS=(u_{k}+v_{k}+1)^2-2(u_{k}+v_{k}+1)-2(u_{k}v_{k}+1)=u_{k+1}+v_{k+1}+1$$

所以由初始条件，
$a_1=u_1+v_1+1=0 \Rightarrow u_1+v_1=-1$，$a_2=u_1\cdot v_1+1=\frac{1}{4} \Rightarrow u_1v_1$.
$u_{1},v_{1}$是方程$t^2+t-\frac{3}{4}=0$的两根，解得$u_{1}=\frac{1}{2}, v_{1}=-\frac{3}{2}$.
由此解出$\phi_{1}=\frac{1}{2} \arccos{\frac{1}{4}},\phi_{2}=\frac{1}{2} \arccos({-\frac{3}{4}})$.
回代即
$$a_n = {
\begin{cases}
2\cos\left(2^{(n+1)/2}\arccos(\frac{1}{4})\right) + 2\cos\left(2^{(n+1)/2}\arccos(-\frac{3}{4})\right) + 1, n为奇数 \\ 
 4\cos\left(2^{n/2}\arccos(\frac{1}{4})\right)\cos\left(2^{n/2}\arccos(-\frac{3}{4})\right) + 1, n为偶数 \\
\end{cases}
}$$
